# Conventions

## Names and paths

- **Journal day:** `Journal/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. **Reviews:** `Journal/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD Weekly Review.md`.
- **Person:** `Love/Rolodex/<Category>/<First Last>.md`.
- **Doctrine:** `DOCTRINES/<Short rule name>.md`.
- **Book or course:** `Wisdom/Books & Courses/<Title> — <Author>.md`.
- **Note titles** are plain words. Avoid `/ \ : * ? " < > |`, which break on Windows.
- **Goals and tasks** are named as outcome + action verb ("Run a half marathon by March"). Record the next physical action.

## Labels

Mark anything not confirmed by the owner or a source:

- `confirmed`: the owner said it, or a document shows it.
- `proposed`: Claude's suggestion, or an idea not yet decided.
- `unverified`: heard somewhere, not checked.

Unlabeled statements in notes the owner wrote are theirs. Claude's additions get a label whenever there is any doubt.

## Voice

- Journal and personal writing stay in the owner's voice: slang, tone, and swearing included. Fix structure or typos only when asked.
- Notes Claude writes are plain and short. Use words the owner would say out loud; no jargon or corporate filler.

## Decisions

When the owner makes a call, write the decision **and the reason** next to it. If the reason isn't known, ask once before writing it down.

## Privacy and secrets

- **Never in the vault:** passwords, API keys, recovery codes, full card or bank numbers, or government ID numbers. Write "in my password manager" instead.
- **If Claude finds a secret:** tell the owner, don't repeat the value in chat, and offer to remove it. If it was ever synced or shared, suggest changing it.
- **Owner-only areas:** Faith & Philosophy, Mental & Emotional Wellbeing, and Romance. Record exactly what the owner says; no interpreting, restructuring, or synthesis unless asked.
- **Nothing leaves the vault** (email, upload, share link, publish, paste into another tool) without explicit OK in the current conversation.

## Safe changes

- **Just do it, then say what changed:** new notes, appending, filing an obvious capture, guide updates, fixing broken links.
- **Ask first:** deleting, merging notes, moving more than a few files, renaming folders, rewriting the owner's words.
- **Bulk changes:** show the plan as a table first, then apply it after the OK.
- **Prefer archiving to deleting.** Move retired notes to `99 Archive/`.
