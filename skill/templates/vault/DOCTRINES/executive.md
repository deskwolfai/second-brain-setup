## Working context

- A doctrine states the principle in the owner's own words, when it applies, what following it looks like, and any exceptions. Keep its source and adoption date.
- A book, author, or framework is not automatically a personal belief. Keep source material in Wisdom and link to it; this folder holds only the rules the owner has adopted.
- Journal can hold the reflection behind a principle; this folder holds the resulting rule. Use the Doctrine template in _Templates.
