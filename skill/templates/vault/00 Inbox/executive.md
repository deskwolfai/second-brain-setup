## TL;DR

## Where to go

## Working context

- Use the inbox when a thought needs capturing and its destination is not obvious yet. Keep enough date or source context to understand it later.
- Dated reflection belongs in Journal, planning belongs in Life Operations, and lasting subject knowledge belongs in the relevant pillar. An inbox item is captured material, not an approved goal or established fact.
- Empty the inbox during a weekly review: file, merge, archive, or delete each item with the owner's go-ahead.

## Keep this guide current

- People and AI agents: read this guide on entry, then check the actual notes before relying on a summary.
- When a folder's purpose, layout, or key notes change, update this guide and any affected parent or child guides. Keep the TL;DR short and the links accurate.
- Keep facts separate from ideas and unknowns. Set Last reviewed after checking the folder.
- Add a dated change-log row describing what changed and why. Keep earlier rows.
